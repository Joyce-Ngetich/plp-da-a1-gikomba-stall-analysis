## Wanjiku's Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded two weeks of daily sales and stock levels in a manual notebook. Without structured insights, she lacks clarity on whether her business is growing, which days generate peak revenue, how her weekday versus weekend performance compares, and which inventory items urgently require restocking versus those yielding the highest gross margins. This analysis uses core Python to deliver actionable, data-driven visibility into her stall's financial and operational performance.

## Top Three Findings
1. **Positive Revenue Growth:** Total sales grew by **10.66%** from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, indicating positive business momentum.
2. **Weekend Dominance:** Weekend average sales (KES 4,900) outperform weekday average sales (KES 2,685) by an impressive **82.49%**, highlighting heavy weekend foot traffic at Gikomba.
3. **Critical Stock Deficits:** Two key produce items—**Tomatoes** (18 kg in stock vs. 25 kg reorder level) and **Sukuma** (9 kg in stock vs. 15 kg reorder level)—have fallen below their reorder thresholds and require immediate restocking.
